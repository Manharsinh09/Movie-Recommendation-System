The Architecture

How The CineMatch AI Engine Works

Our machine learning pipeline transforms raw plot metadata into real-time semantic affinity scores.

STEP 01
📊

TF-IDF Vectorization

We tokenize plot summaries, genre tags, and cast fingerprints into multi-dimensional sparse vector representations, filtering out linguistic noise.

STEP 02
📐

Cosine Vector Geometry

The query movie vector is measured against 4,800+ films using normalized dot-product angles to uncover the mathematically closest cinematic matches.

STEP 03
⚡

OMDb Live Enrichment

Top candidates are asynchronously enriched in milliseconds with high-resolution posters, verified IMDb scores, cast lists, and runtimes.

Frequently Asked Questions

TF-IDF (Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency) measures how unique and important descriptive keywords are across our film database. We compute the Cosine Similarity angle between the queried film vector and all 4,800+ movies in N-dimensional space to rank identical thematic matches.
